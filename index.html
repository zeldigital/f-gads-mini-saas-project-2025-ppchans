The H.A.N.S. Framework

Four layers every engagement runs through — in order, every time. Strategy before spend, signal before scale.

H
High-leverage strategy

Offer, market, ICP, budget, and channel decisions locked before a single dollar is committed. Every campaign launches with a written brief — no guessing mid-flight.

A
Accurate signal

Server-side tracking, CRM data, first-party audiences, and conversion-quality filters — so the bidding algorithm works from clean data, not inflated numbers.

N
AI-native execution

AI handles research, creative variants, audits, and anomaly detection. Every output goes through human review before it ships. Speed without blind trust.

S
Scale with guardrails

Lead-quality thresholds, margin constraints, brand safety rules, and automation overrides set before spend increases. Scaling should never surprise you.
